Shampoo Ingredients Guide

Know what ingredients work best for your hair type

Oily Scalp

✓ Look For:
  • Salicylic Acid / Tea Tree Oil
  • Clarifying surfactants
  • Lightweight formulas
✕ Avoid:
  • Heavy silicones and oils
  • Extremely drying sulfates

Dry Scalp

✓ Look For:
  • Glycerin / Aloe Vera
  • Gentle sulfate-free formulas
  • Soothing botanicals
✕ Avoid:
  • Harsh sulfates
  • Drying alcohols (isopropyl alcohol)
