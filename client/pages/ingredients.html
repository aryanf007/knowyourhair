Shampoo Ingredients Guide

Learn what ingredients work best for your hair type

By Hair Porosity

Low Porosity

✅ Look For:

  • Lightweight conditioners
  • Gentle surfactants (cocamidopropyl betaine)
  • Water-based products
  • Humectants (glycerin, aloe)
  • Heat-activated ingredients

❌ Avoid:

  • Heavy silicones (dimethicone, amodimethicone)
  • Heavy butters and oils
  • Protein-heavy formulas
  • Thick, creamy conditioners

Medium Porosity

✅ Look For:

  • Balanced humectants
  • Light proteins (hydrolyzed wheat protein)
  • Moisture-protein balance
  • Versatile formulas
  • Moderate conditioning agents

💡 Tips:

  • You can use a variety of products
  • Focus on maintaining balance
  • Alternate between protein and moisture
  • Avoid extremes in either direction

High Porosity

✅ Look For:

  • Moisture + protein combination
  • Sealing ingredients (oils, butters)
  • Gentle but effective surfactants
  • Deep conditioning agents
  • Strengthening proteins

💡 Tips:

  • Focus on both moisture and protein
  • Seal in moisture after washing
  • Use strengthening treatments regularly
  • Avoid over-washing

By Scalp Type

Oily Scalp

✅ Look For:

  • Clarifying but non-stripping surfactants
  • Niacinamide
  • Green tea extract
  • Salicylic acid (gentle)
  • Tea tree oil
  • Lightweight formulas

❌ Avoid:

  • Co-washes/no-shampoo
  • Overly moisturizing formulas
  • Harsh sulfates (can cause rebound oiliness)
  • Heavy conditioners on scalp

Dry Scalp

✅ Look For:

  • Aloe vera
  • Hyaluronic acid
  • Fatty alcohols (cetyl, stearyl)
  • Gentle, moisturizing surfactants
  • Soothing ingredients (chamomile, calendula)
  • Light oils (jojoba, argan)

❌ Avoid:

  • Harsh sulfates
  • Drying alcohols (ethanol, isopropyl)
  • Over-clarifying formulas
  • Fragrance-heavy products

Reading Ingredient Labels

Key Points:

  • Ingredients are listed by concentration (highest first)
  • First 5 ingredients are most important
  • Look for natural vs. synthetic options
  • Avoid known irritants if you have sensitive skin

Common Ingredients:

  • Surfactants: Clean hair (SLS, cocamidopropyl betaine)
  • Humectants: Attract moisture (glycerin, hyaluronic acid)
  • Emollients: Soften hair (oils, butters)
  • Proteins: Strengthen hair (keratin, wheat protein)
