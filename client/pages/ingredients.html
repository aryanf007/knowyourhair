Hair Product Ingredient Decoder

Understand labels so you can choose safe, nourishing products for your hair type.

Beneficial

Aloe Vera & Glycerin

Category: Humectants

Draws moisture from the air directly into the hair shaft. Excellent for dry hair and curly textures.

Use with Caution

Sulfates (SLS / SLES)

Category: Harsh Cleansers

Strip hair of natural oils, leading to severe dryness and color fading over time.

Beneficial

Hydrolyzed Keratin / Silk

Category: Proteins

Fills in tiny cuticle gaps in high-porosity or damaged hair to restore structural strength.

Use with Caution

Non-Soluble Silicones

Category: Coating Agents (Dimethicone)

Provides temporary shine but seals out moisture, causing build-up without clarifying shampoo.
