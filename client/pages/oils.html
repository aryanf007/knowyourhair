Hair Oils Guide

Find the perfect oils for your hair porosity type

🌊 Low Porosity

Requires lightweight oils that won't sit on the surface.

Jojoba Oil

Lightweight, mimics natural sebum

Grapeseed Oil

Very light, non-greasy

Argan Oil

Lightweight, rich in vitamins

⚖️ Medium Porosity

Balanced hair can handle a variety of oils.

Sweet Almond Oil

Balanced, good moisture retention

Avocado Oil

Nourishing, medium weight

Sunflower Oil

Lightweight, versatile

💧 High Porosity

Needs heavier sealing oils to lock in moisture.

Castor Oil

Heavy, excellent for sealing

Olive Oil

Rich, deeply moisturizing

Shea Butter / Oil

Heavy barrier, great for sealing
