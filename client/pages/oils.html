Hair Oils Guide

Find the perfect oils for your hair porosity type

🌊 Low Porosity

Your hair needs lightweight oils that won't cause buildup.

Recommended Oils:
💧 Jojoba Oil Lightweight, mimics natural sebum
🍇 Grapeseed Oil Very light, non-greasy
🌿 Argan Oil Lightweight, rich in nutrients
⚠️ Avoid: Heavy oils like castor, olive, or shea butter – they can cause buildup on low porosity hair.

⚖️ Medium Porosity

Your hair can handle a balanced variety of oils.

Recommended Oils:
🥥 Coconut Oil (Light) Balanced, good for most hair types
🌰 Almond Oil Nourishing, medium weight
🌻 Sunflower Oil Lightweight, versatile
💡 Tip: You can experiment with different oils to find what works best for your hair.

💧 High Porosity

Your hair needs heavier sealing oils to lock in moisture.

Recommended Oils:
🫒 Castor Oil Heavy, excellent for sealing
🌿 Olive Oil Rich, deeply moisturizing
🧈 Shea-Based Oils Heavy butter, great for sealing
💡 Tip: Mix heavy oils with lighter ones for easier application. Always seal in moisture after conditioning.

General Oil Usage Tips

When to Apply:

  • On damp hair for better absorption
  • Before washing (pre-poo treatment)
  • After conditioning (seal in moisture)
  • As a weekly deep treatment

How to Apply:

  • Start with small amounts
  • Focus on ends and mid-lengths
  • Avoid roots (unless treating dryness)
  • Use heat for better penetration
