Our Mission

KnowYourHair is dedicated to helping you discover your hair's unique needs and embrace natural care for healthy, beautiful hair. We believe that understanding your hair's porosity, scalp type, and specific concerns is the first step toward a personalized care routine that works.

What We Offer

• Hair Porosity Tests: Simple tests to determine your hair's porosity level.
• Scalp Analysis: Guidance to identify your scalp type.
• Personalized Routines: AI-generated hair care routines tailored to your specific needs.

Important Disclaimer: We are not dermatologists. This website provides general information and educational content about hair care. For serious scalp or hair issues, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
