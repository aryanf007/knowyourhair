Scalp Analysis

Determine your scalp type with these simple tests

🔍 Visual Cues Observe your scalp's appearance
👆 Touch Test Feel your scalp's texture
🧪 Sebum Test Check oil production

Normal Scalp

Visual Cues:

  • Balanced appearance
  • No excessive oiliness or dryness
  • Healthy shine overall
  • Minimal flaking

Touch Test:

  • Feels smooth and balanced
  • Not overly oily or dry
  • Comfortable to touch
Care Tips:
  • Wash 2–3 times per week
  • Use balanced, gentle shampoos
  • Maintain regular routine
  • Look for: Balanced formulas, mild surfactants

Dry Scalp

Visual Cues:

  • Visible flaking or dandruff
  • Tight, dry appearance
  • May appear red or irritated
  • Small white flakes

Touch Test:

  • Feels tight or itchy
  • Lack of moisture when touched
  • May feel rough
Care Tips:
  • Wash less frequently (1–2x/week)
  • Use moisturizing, gentle shampoos
  • Pre-oil before washing
  • Look for: Hyaluronic acid, fatty alcohols
  • Avoid harsh sulfates

Oily Scalp

Visual Cues:

  • Shiny, greasy appearance
  • Hair gets greasy quickly after washing
  • May have larger, yellowish flakes
  • Clogged pores

Touch Test:

  • Feels greasy or oily
  • Oil visible on fingers
  • Slippery to touch
Care Tips:
  • Wash 3–4 times per week
  • Use clarifying but non-stripping shampoo
  • Focus on scalp cleansing
  • Look for: Niacinamide, green tea, gentle clarifying agents
  • Avoid heavy oils on scalp

Sebum Test Instructions

  1. Wash your hair and wait 24 hours.
  2. Take a tissue or blotting paper.
  3. Gently press it against your scalp (especially crown area).
  4. Observe the results.
Results:
  • No oil: Dry Scalp
  • Light oil: Normal Scalp
  • Visible oil: Oily Scalp
