Common Hair Care Mistakes

Avoid these common pitfalls for healthier hair

⚠️ Using Too Much Oil

Applying excessive amounts of oil can lead to product buildup, greasiness, and clogged pores on the scalp.

Fix: Start with small amounts (a few drops) and focus on ends and mid-lengths.

💧 Overwashing

Washing your hair too frequently can strip natural oils, leading to scalp dryness and rebound oil production.

Fix: Wash based on your scalp type (2-3x/week for normal/oily, 1-2x for dry).

🧴 Applying Conditioner on Scalp

Conditioner directly on the scalp clogs pores and leads to limp, greasy roots.

Fix: Apply conditioner from mid-lengths to ends only.
